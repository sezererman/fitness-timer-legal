# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

At Fonksiyonel Fitness Timer ("we," "our," or "us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and protected when you use the Fonksiyonel Fitness Timer mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the data practices described in this policy. This document complies with the GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) and Turkey’s KVKK standards.

## 1. Information We Collect

To provide and improve our services, we may collect the following types of data:

- **Account Information:** The email address you provide when registering. If you choose to sign in via Apple or Google, we receive basic profile information (name and email) from those platforms.
- **User-Generated Content (UGC):** Workout logs, times, notes, likes, and comments that you share within the community.
- **Device and Usage Data:** Your device operating system, IP address, and in-app usage statistics (e.g., timer usage durations).

*(Note: Our App does not collect or process sensitive health data such as heart rate, step count, or weight from HealthKit or Google Fit. It functions solely as a timer and workout-logging tool.)*

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect for the following purposes:

- To create, manage, and authenticate your account (using the Supabase infrastructure).
- To provide core functionality (timers, workout logs).
- To enable community features (sharing workouts and interacting with other users).
- To improve the user experience, detect bugs, and enhance the App.
- To enforce our Terms of Use (e.g., blocking abusive users or inappropriate content) and comply with legal obligations.

## 3. Device Permissions

The App may require the following permissions to function correctly:

- **Wake Lock (Keep Screen On):** To prevent your device screen from turning off while a timer is running.
- **Vibration:** To provide haptic feedback during timer phase transitions.
- **Audio:** To play timer whistles and notifications while the App is running in the background.
These permissions are used strictly to ensure the timer operates properly.

## 4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. However, we may share information in the following circumstances:

- **Service Providers:** We use **Supabase** for authentication, database management, and server infrastructure. Supabase processes and protects your data using high security standards.
- **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose your information to competent authorities when required by law.

## 5. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us. Your account information and passwords are encrypted and stored within the Supabase infrastructure. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 6. Your Rights (GDPR & KVKK)

Under applicable legal regulations, you have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- Request the deletion of your data (Right to be Forgotten).
- Object to the processing of your data.

To delete your account and data, or to exercise any of your other rights, you can use the "Delete Account" option in the in-app settings at any time or contact us at ermansezer@zohomail.com.
